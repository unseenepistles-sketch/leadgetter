Before You Message

Quick answers.

How do I pay?

Pay on delivery within our delivery zone — inspect the product, then pay by cash or M-Pesa. For sourced or out-of-zone orders we confirm terms on WhatsApp first.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes — Nairobi is same/next-day, and we ship countrywide via courier. Message us your location and we'll confirm the option and timing.

How long does custom sourcing take?

Most sourced products land in roughly 2–4 weeks depending on item and origin. You get a landed, all-in quote before we order.

What if the product arrives damaged?

You inspect before you pay, so you never accept a damaged item. If a fault develops later, message us — we replace or refund per the terms confirmed at order.

What's the minimum order for wholesale?

Wholesale tiers vary by product. Tell us the item and volume and we'll send the bulk pricing.

Ready to get the good stuff?

Order a product, ask for wholesale pricing, or send us something to source. One message is all it takes.
